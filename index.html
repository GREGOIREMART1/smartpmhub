AI for project management

Where AI actually creates value in delivery — and where it doesn't.

The hype says "AI everywhere." The value is narrower and clearer: give project teams back the hours lost to copy-paste, so they spend their time on analysis and decisions. Here's how I think about it.

→

One principle: frictionless, but governed.

The target is a way of working where project managers spend their time on judgement, not on assembling reports by hand. Two things have to be true first: the data has to be good enough to trust, and the capability has to be built through structured training — not left to chance. AI without those two is a demo, not a delivery.

Category 01

Turn talk into artefacts

The highest-value, lowest-risk wins: let AI draft the things PMs retype by hand.

  • Meeting transcript → status or closure report
  • Conversational queries over your portfolio data
  • Structured, guided project intake
Category 02

Watch the portfolio for you

More diffuse, more powerful over time — AI that reads across the portfolio so nothing slips.

  • Cross-cutting scan of risk & issue logs
  • Automatic data enrichment and de-duplication
  • Anomaly detection on project data
Category 03

Build the capability

Tools without trained people fail. AI literacy has to be part of how PMs are developed.

  • AI modules built into project-management training
  • Role-based learning paths, not generic links
  • A named go-to expert per team

Quick wins first.

Not every use case is worth doing now. I score each on business impact and ease of implementation — and start where impact is high and effort is low, before touching anything that needs real budget or custom build.

Quick wins — start here

  • AI literacy built into existing PM training
