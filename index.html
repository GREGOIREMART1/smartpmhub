What I believe

Three convictions that shape every engagement.

01

Governance makes acceleration safe

Structure is what lets you move fast without shadow-AI or shadow-project risk. The framework isn't the brake — it's the guardrail that lets you take the corner faster.

02

Economics beat the demo

Build-vs-buy, licensing models, and total cost decide whether value is real. A dazzling proof-of-concept that doesn't survive its own business case was never a solution.

03

Adoption is the only metric that counts

A framework nobody uses is a cost, not an asset. I measure success by what teams actually keep doing — not by what was delivered on paper.

How it works

A disciplined path from ambition to adopted delivery.

Three phases, each with a clear deliverable. You keep ownership throughout — the goal is to leave your teams running the machine.

Phase 01

Diagnose

We start with how your organisation actually delivers today — the governance gaps, the bottlenecks, the shadow work. You get a clear-eyed maturity assessment, not a sales pitch.

Phase 02

Design the governed roadmap

I come back with a prioritised plan: where to intervene, what it costs, what it returns, and how AI fits safely into the operating model. Decisions are grounded in economics, not the demo.

Phase 03

Stand it up & hand it over

We deploy the framework alongside your teams and build the capability to sustain it. Success is measured in adoption — what people keep doing after I've gone.
